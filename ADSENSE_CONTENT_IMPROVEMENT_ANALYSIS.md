### Content Examples to Implement

#### Example 1: Expanded FAQ Answer
**Current:** "Refresh rate is the number of times your display updates per second."

**Improved:** "Refresh rate, measured in Hertz (Hz), represents how many times per second your monitor can display a new image. A 60Hz monitor refreshes 60 times per second, while a 144Hz monitor refreshes 144 times per second. This directly impacts how smooth motion appears on your screen.

Higher refresh rates provide several benefits:
- Smoother gaming experience with reduced motion blur
- Better responsiveness in competitive gaming
- Reduced eye strain during extended use
- Improved visual clarity in fast-moving scenes

However, to benefit from higher refresh rates, your graphics card must be capable of generating frames at that rate. A 144Hz monitor won't improve your experience if your GPU can only produce 60 frames per second in your games."
